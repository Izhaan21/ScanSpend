# Privacy Policy for ScanSpend

**Last updated: August 31, 2026**

ScanSpend ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ScanSpend mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

---

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you register or log in, we collect:
- Your **email address**
- Your **name** (if provided or from your Google account)

### Receipt Data
When you scan a receipt, the image is processed to extract:
- Merchant name, date, items, and total amount

Receipt images are processed by Google's AI services and are not permanently stored on our servers.

### Location Data
With your permission, we may access your approximate location solely to auto-detect your local currency. This data is not stored or shared.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your account
- Process and store your scanned receipt data
- Display your expense history and analytics
- Auto-detect your local currency

---

## Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our backend servers hosted by Render. We use industry-standard HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between the App and our servers. Your authentication credentials are stored securely using encrypted storage on your device.

---

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:
- **Google Sign-In** – for authentication
- **Google ML Kit** – for on-device text recognition from receipt images
- **Google Gemini AI** – for intelligent receipt data extraction
- **Firebase** – for authentication infrastructure

---

## Data Retention

We retain your account and receipt data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

---

## Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
