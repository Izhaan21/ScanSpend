# Precision Scan Design System

## Brand & Style
The design system is anchored in the concept of "Digital Assurance." It targets professionals and business owners who require speed without sacrificing accuracy. The visual language balances high-tech efficiency with institutional reliability.

## Colors
- **Primary (Deep Navy):** `#0F172A`
- **Secondary (Vibrant Teal):** `#0D9488`
- **Background (Soft Grey):** `#F8FAFC`
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Error:** `#BA1A1A`
